# ECE NTUA Retrospection ⚡

## Motivation

Computers are surpassing our abilities in programming and math by the moment.
Our field is changing at a foundational level with unprecedented speed.
Writing code by hand or trying to tackle a difficult problem without the
machine's assistance will be neither efficient nor economically profitable.
From one perspective, these changes are going to elevate our productivity into
new heights. But from another, they will deprive us from the things that, for
a lot of us, have driven our passion for science from the beginning.
Trying to solve a problem for hours - even days, friction with proofs, stubborn bugs,
failing code are all shared experiences that made every all-nighter, every eureka moment,
every solution a small triumph.
A trophy of joy that not only made every hardship worth it, but furthermore
inspired the pursuit of the next goal. 

This approach on our field's challenges is maybe losing a lot of its "value", but not its meaning.
It never will. 

## Content

Our school brought us in contact with a vast number of ideas, methods and concepts from a variety of
fields that can either be implemented using code, or displayed through a pdf document. The repository
contains 9 directories - one for every semester. Each one can be subdivided into subjects.
Those will contain handwritten code or text about a topic that was covered in the particular subject.
Every addition must be documented into the subject directory's README.md file, ideally with a link.
All PRs will be accepted, unless there is unnoticed full repetition of a previously uploaded work.
They do not have to be flawless or frequent. Humans make mistakes and sometimes lack consistency. 
There will not be any attempt on verification of absence of AI generated content. It defies the purpose. 

## Conclusion

This repo is not an attempt to fight the future. This has always been proven to be a bad
and shortsighted idea. It aspires to be a portal through which we can relive what gave us a
purpose. A playground for our free time, the place where we get in touch with our good old
habits without the pressure of deadlines and lack of results.


Don't stop sharing your creativity and sharpening your skills.
Our thoughts will always matter and every commit is going to be the proof.
